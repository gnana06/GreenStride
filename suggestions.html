💡 Sustainability Suggestions

🚲 Use a Bicycle Reduce fuel consumption and lower carbon emissions.
💧 Save Water Turn off taps while brushing and repair water leaks.
🛍 Use Reusable Bags Avoid single-use plastics and reduce waste generation.
🌳 Plant Trees Trees absorb carbon dioxide and improve air quality.
🔌 Save Electricity Switch off unused lights, fans, and electronic devices.
♻️ Recycle Waste Separate recyclable materials from general household waste.
🥗 Reduce Food Waste Plan meals carefully and donate excess food whenever possible.
☀️ Use Natural Light Make use of sunlight during the day to save energy.
